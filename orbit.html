Solar System Orbit Simulation

An educational VR environment built in Unity to make orbital mechanics, planetary motion, axial tilt, and seasons easier to explore spatially.

UNITY 2022.3 LTSC#META QUEST · OPENXR · STEAMVR

The goal

Orbital mechanics can be difficult to understand from equations and static diagrams alone. The project translates these concepts into an immersive environment where students can explore the solar system and observe motion in 3D.

Experience

  • Interactive solar-system exploration and planet close-ups
  • Floating 2D task tablet for learning activities
  • Seasons simulation showing Earth rotation and seasonal change
  • Red Sun interactive activity
  • Oculus, SteamVR, and OpenXR workflows

Technical work

The simulation uses Unity, C#, Rigidbody physics, and XR tooling. The project also translated orbital research and MATLAB-based scientific work into a C# environment suitable for an interactive educational application.

Why VR?

VR is useful here because spatial relationships are part of the subject itself, not simply decoration around the content.
